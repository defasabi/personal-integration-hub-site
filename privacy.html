Privacy Policy

Personal Integration Hub
Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Overview

Personal Integration Hub is a privately operated software project that connects authorized applications and services through Google APIs. It is intended for use by its developer and is not offered as a public service.

2. Information Accessed

Depending on the integration, the software may access information from Google Calendar and Google Sheets. Access is limited to permissions authorized through Google OAuth.

3. Use of Information

Information is processed to support authorized synchronization, data management, and related automation. Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, or training artificial intelligence models.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data may be stored on systems controlled by the developer and in services involved in authorized integrations. OAuth credentials and sensitive configuration information are not intentionally made publicly accessible. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored information and credentials.

5. Data Sharing

Information may be transmitted between services as necessary to perform authorized integrations. Google user data is not sold or shared with advertisers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained as necessary for the operation of integrations and may be deleted from systems controlled by the developer when no longer required.

7. Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including applicable Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the software or its data handling practices.
